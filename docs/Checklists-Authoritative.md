# Checklists — Authoritative SSOT

**Source:** Original specifications received from the CEO  
**Status:** Authoritative. Do not alter wording.  
**Date recorded:** 10 November 2026

These lists distinguish usage context:
- **Directivo** — used by the CEO when presenting to the Board.
- **Operativo** — used by the CEO for performance assessment meetings (weekly, daily, or in between).

Command Centers serve the Operativo lists. Directivo items are out of scope for the department command-center pages unless explicitly added later.
